# **The Gilded Wake — Game Design Document**

A point-and-click adventure comedy set in a soot-kissed coastal world where impossible relics from an ancient civilization keep washing ashore. The junk dealers call them "storm junk." Priests call them "metal omens." Customs calls them "undeclared hazard spheres."

The protagonist discovers these relics react to them personally, revealing flashes of memory and pointing toward a hidden island where a lost utopian civilization still survives.

---

# 1. Opening Scene Script

## **Opening Location: Blackwake Harbor at Dawn**

A crooked harbor town of brass pipes, tide-worn brick, leaning cranes, and hissing steam vents. Fishing boats bob beside salvage barges. Gulls scream overhead like underpaid theater critics. Strange pieces of polished, seamless metal lie tangled in seaweed along the shore.

### **Opening Visual**

A storm has just passed. The tide has left behind an enormous smooth black object embedded in the sand, half shell, half machine, faintly glowing with geometric lines. Around it stands a crowd of opportunists, mystics, and municipal officials arguing over ownership.

### **Characters in Scene**

* **Rowan Vale**: protagonist
* **Tibbit Wrench**: tinkerer, scavenger, self-certified genius
* **Dockmaster Pindle**: absurd bureaucrat
* **Crowd**
* **Ancient Device**: technically not a character, but behaving like one already

## **First Puzzle Introduction: Activate the Relic**

### Goal

Figure out how to safely activate the ancient device.

### Visible Interactables

* The ancient relic
* Rowan's medallion
* A cracked spyglass in a salvage crate
* Dockmaster's inspection stamp
* A steam valve connected to a nearby crane
* A warning placard: "NO UNAUTHORIZED RESONANCE EVENTS"

### Puzzle Logic

The relic's indentation is full of wet sand. Rowan cannot insert the medallion until it's cleaned. The obvious move, using hands, fails because the indentation is too delicate and the device zaps them lightly. The solution involves:

1. Use the cracked spyglass to focus morning sunlight and dry the sand crust
2. Use Dockmaster's oversized inspection stamp handle as a scraping tool
3. Insert the medallion
4. Open the steam valve to power the crane horn, producing a resonance tone that fully wakes the relic

### Funny Wrong Answers

* **Use hand on relic**:
  **ROWAN**: I'm not saying it bit me. I'm saying we now have a hostile understanding.
* **Ask Pindle for help**:
  **PINDLE**: I only interfere when things are safe, dull, and already complete.
* **Kick relic**:
  **ROWAN**: That was for confidence. Mine, not its.

### Puzzle Payoff

When activated, the device projects a map fragment and Rowan gets a memory flash:
A white shoreline. Gleaming towers. Voices saying, "Find the Wake Road before the gates close."

Rowan collapses briefly.

**TIBBIT**
You all right?

**ROWAN**
No, but in a promising way.

# 2. Main Characters

## **1. Rowan Vale, the Protagonist**

### Personality

Dryly funny, observant, skeptical, more emotionally guarded than they seem. Has an instinctive connection to ancient technology they do not understand.

### Speaking Style

Short, sharp lines. Understated sarcasm. More clever when uncomfortable.

### Sample Lines

* "I prefer my mysteries smaller and less likely to hum at me."
* "That explanation raised more questions than it solved, which I assume was your goal."
* "I have the unsettling feeling my childhood omitted several important footnotes."

### Comedic Quirk

Always tries the practical solution first, but the universe keeps rewarding nonsense.

---

## **2. Tibbit Wrench, Inventor Companion**

### Personality

Chaotic, enthusiastic, brilliant in bursts, catastrophically overconfident. Thinks every problem improves with pressure valves.

### Speaking Style

Fast, overstuffed, technical nonsense with occasional accidental genius.

### Sample Lines

* "Good news. I know exactly what this is. Better news, I have three mutually exclusive theories."
* "It's not unstable. It's dynamically opinionated."
* "I once built a tea kettle that could predict rain. Admittedly it predicted rain every day, but eventually it was correct."

### Comedic Quirk

Names every invention as if unveiling a royal battleship.

---

## **3. Commodore Silas Rook, Rival/Antagonist**

### Personality

Charming, polished, ruthless. A salvage magnate who masks greed as civic progress. Believes lost technology belongs in the hands of those "capable" enough to wield it.

### Speaking Style

Silky, theatrical, impeccably phrased insults.

### Sample Lines

* "History belongs to those with the discipline to curate it."
* "You mistake inheritance for understanding."
* "The world does not need wonder, Miss Vale. It needs management."

### Comedic Quirk

Always arrives dramatically, even when it is logistically absurd.

---

## **4. Marrow Quill, the Mysterious Guide**

### Personality

Reserved, sly, melancholy. Seems to know ancient symbols and the truth about Rowan, but reveals information in maddening fragments.

### Speaking Style

Poetic, elliptical, annoyingly cryptic.

### Sample Lines

* "Some doors open with keys. Others open when they recognize the hand that made them."
* "The sea returns what the world is ready to remember."
* "You are not being followed by fate. You are being recalled."

### Comedic Quirk

Appears in impossible places and refuses to explain how.

---

## **5. Dockmaster Pindle, Absurd Side Character**

### Personality

Petty, rule-obsessed, timid until paperwork empowers him.

### Speaking Style

Formally ridiculous. Loves naming bylaws.

### Sample Lines

* "That is a Category Seven irregularity with decorative edges."
* "Adventure requires a filing fee."
* "You may not enter the restricted zone without a stamped waiver, a witness, and a second stamp verifying the first stamp."

### Comedic Quirk

Carries stamps for situations no one has ever encountered.

---

## **6. Optional Bonus Character: Mirelle Soot**

A junk market trader with a talent for finding impossible parts and charging impossible prices.

### Sample Lines

* "I don't sell junk. I sell unloved futures."
* "Yes, it's cursed. That's extra."

# 3. Puzzle Design

## **Puzzle 1: Wake the Shore Relic**

Already described in opening scene.

## **Puzzle 2: Get Past Harbor Customs**

### Setup

Pindle blocks access to the salvage warehouse where more relics are stored.

### Required Items

* Blank customs form
* Ink pad
* Fish wrapping paper
* Fake seal made from carved potato or soap

### Logic

Rowan must create an official-looking "Emergency Relic Moisture Inspection Authorization."

### Solution

1. Steal wording from harbor signs
2. Use fish paper to make it smell "administrative"
3. Stamp it with a forged seal
4. Present it with extreme confidence

### Joke Payoff

Pindle approves it instantly because it has too many stamps to be questioned.

**PINDLE**
This is clearly legitimate. No forgery has ever shown such commitment to procedure.

---

## **Puzzle 3: The Literal Lift**

### Setup

A freight lift only responds to spoken commands, but the ancient voice interface interprets everything literally.

### Required Items

* Phrasebook
* Brass speaking cone
* Chalk notes from earlier failed attempts

### Logic

The lift misinterprets ordinary requests.

### Examples

* "Take me down" causes melancholic music
* "Lift up" makes it compliment Rowan's posture
* "Open" gives a lecture on emotional vulnerability

### Solution

Find the exact ancient command phrase, roughly translated as:
**"Change vertical priority: lower."**

### Joke Payoff

**LIFT VOICE**
Vertical priority changed. Your phrasing remains poor.

---

## **Puzzle 4: Reassemble the Memory Lens**

### Setup

An ancient lens in the old lighthouse can replay memory echoes, but it's missing parts scavenged across town.

### Required Items

* Polished monocle glass
* Clock spring
* Conductive sea salt paste
* Lens housing from a music box

### Logic

Combine weirdly mismatched items to create a functioning optical resonator.

### Solution

Use Tibbit to assemble it, but only after distracting him from "improving" it with explosive enhancements.

### Joke Payoff

The lens works, but Tibbit adds a little whistle.

**ROWAN**
Why does memory whistle now?

**TIBBIT**
Atmosphere.

---

## **Puzzle 5: The Market Haggling Duel**

### Setup

Mirelle Soot has a key component but refuses coin. She wants something "rarer than value."

### Required Items

* Embarrassing secret from another NPC
* Fancy teacup
* Fake aristocratic accent
* Broken but prestigious machine badge

### Logic

This is a dialogue puzzle where Rowan must convincingly pretend the broken badge is from an elite guild.

### Solution

Learn the guild jargon from eavesdropping, then bluff.

### Funny Failure

If done badly:

**MIRELLE**
That accent started in the capital and died in a chimney.

---

## **Puzzle 6: Outwit the Pneumatic Archivist**

### Setup

An automated library machine only dispenses knowledge if questions are filed properly.

### Required Items

* Query cards
* Numbering code
* Ancient symbol index

### Logic

Need to ask the right question in the machine's bizarre filing syntax.

### Solution

Instead of "Where is the island?" the correct query is:
**"Transit routes, concealed civic continuities, offshore."**

### Joke Payoff

The machine responds:
**"At last. Someone literate enough to deserve catastrophe."**

---

## **Puzzle 7: Airship Ignition by Choir**

### Setup

An ancient engine in a derelict airship starts only when harmonics match a stored pattern.

### Required Items

* Steam organ
* Tin whistle
* Harbor bell
* Three NPCs recruited to hold notes badly

### Logic

The player arranges a ridiculous improvised orchestra.

### Solution

Use environmental sounds to match the engine's chord pattern.

### Joke Payoff

The ship launches with majestic grandeur while everyone sings off-key.

---

## **Puzzle 8: The Fog Ruins Shadow Door**

### Setup

A door in the ruins opens only when shadows align with engraved constellations.

### Required Items

* Lantern
* Gearwork bird statue
* Umbrella skeleton
* Reflective foil

### Logic

Manipulate light and object silhouettes to form the missing ancient symbol.

### Joke Payoff

Wrong combinations create silly shadow animals, prompting commentary from Rowan.

**ROWAN**
We are either opening a sacred gateway or entertaining a very selective toddler.

---

## **Puzzle 9: The Truth Engine**

### Setup

A machine reveals genetic memory, but only to someone wearing "the face of home."

### Required Items

* Rowan's medallion
* White powder to reveal hidden engraving
* Fabric from Rowan's childhood coat
* Conductive bracelet from relic

### Logic

Player discovers the medallion contains a tiny encoded crest matching the utopian island.

### Payoff

Big story reveal: Rowan was sent away as a child when the island sealed itself.

---

## **Puzzle 10: Final Gate to the Island**

### Setup

The sea path to the island is invisible except when ancient relay towers are activated in sequence.

### Required Items

* Map fragments
* Charged relic core
* Lens alignment chart
* Airship

### Logic

A grand final multi-step puzzle tying prior clues together.

### Payoff

The fog parts, revealing a brilliant island of clean white stone, quiet canals, impossible gardens, and silent machines that move like choreography.

**ROWAN**
I crossed half the world for answers.

**TIBBIT**
And?

**ROWAN**
It appears the answer is that my people had extremely strong opinions about architecture.

# 5. Ancient Technology System

## **What It Actually Is**

Remnants of an advanced pre-collapse civilization that mastered energy resonance, material memory, and long-range concealed transit systems. Their devices are not magical, but so sophisticated they are mistaken for divine artifacts.

## **Why It Was Lost**

The civilization chose isolation during a global age of warfare and exploitation. They sealed their island routes and erased most access points rather than let their tools become weapons.

## **Why It's Resurfacing Now**

The sea barriers and relay systems are failing after centuries. Storms are uncovering buried infrastructure and washing fragments back into the world.

## **How It Connects to Rowan**

Rowan was born on the island. As a child, they were sent away during an internal crisis involving succession, ideology, or a disagreement over whether the island should remain hidden. Their medallion is both an identity token and route key.

## **Technology Rules**

* Ancient tech responds to resonance patterns, not crude force
* It recognizes lineage, symbols, and specific encoded materials
* It is highly literal in interface design
* It prefers harmonious inputs: sound, alignment, pattern, memory signatures
* Damage from misuse tends to be inconvenient and embarrassing rather than apocalyptic, until late-game

# 6. Humor and Running Gags

## **Running Gag 1: Bureaucracy Beats Reality**

Pindle keeps inventing forms for increasingly impossible situations.

* "Unauthorized prophecy declaration"
* "Temporary relic dripping permit"
* "Aggressive glowing incident record"

## **Running Gag 2: Ancient Machines Are Too Literal**

Interfaces interpret commands exactly.

* "Show me the way" becomes a spotlight pointed at Rowan's shoes
* "I need a hand" produces a metal hand

## **Running Gag 3: Tibbit's Inventions Have Prestige Names**

* The Imperial Atmospheric Persuader
* The Portable Coil of Selective Certainty
* The New Tibbit Wrench Mark III, Revised and Less Legally Actionable

## **Running Gag 4: No One Agrees What Relics Are**

Magic, treasure, dangerous scrap, divine judgment, luxury decor

## **Running Gag 5: Rowan's Item Descriptions**

Everything inspected gets a dry remark.

* **Rusty valve**: "A wheel for deciding whether pipes are having a good day."
* **Relic shard**: "Either priceless history or very arrogant cutlery."
* **Official stamp**: "The preferred weapon of small authority."

# 7. Story Arc

## **Beginning**

A relic washes ashore and reacts to Rowan. Rowan and Tibbit begin investigating while Rook takes interest. The first clues suggest Rowan's past is tied to the relics.

## **Middle**

The pair gather map fragments, repair devices, and visit ruins. Marrow Quill hints Rowan was not abandoned, but hidden. Rook grows more active, seizing relics and trying to force interfaces open.

## **Twist**

Rowan is not merely descended from the lost civilization. They are from it. Sent away deliberately with a key tied to the island's sealed route system. The island still exists, and some there may have expected Rowan's return.

## **Climax**

Rook reaches the final transit chamber too, intending to claim the island's technology. Rowan must solve the gate puzzle before he brute-forces entry and damages the system permanently.

## **Ending**

The path opens. Rowan reaches Isle Auric and discovers the utopia is real, but not simple. It thrives, yet at the cost of isolation. Rowan's arrival may force it to rejoin the wider world, whether it wants to or not.

# 8. Dialogue Style and Optional Trees

## **Object Examination Examples**

**Ancient relay node**
"Smooth, elegant, and entirely unwilling to explain itself. I respect that, to a point."

**Harbor permit rack**
"Each folder contains a different way to be told no."

**Steam crane**
"A monument to industry, overengineering, and the local belief that levers are a personality."

---

## **Sample Dialogue Tree: Talking to Pindle**

### Ask about the relic

**ROWAN**: What do you think it is?
**PINDLE**: Officially? Unclassified maritime inconvenience.
**ROWAN**: And unofficially?
**PINDLE**: Unclassified maritime inconvenience with paperwork.

### Ask for access to warehouse

**PINDLE**: Absolutely not.

* **"What if it's urgent?"**
  "Then it requires urgent denial."
* **"What if I'm authorized?"**
  "That would be upsetting."
* **"What if I go in anyway?"**
  "Then I shall write a letter of such severity."

---

## **Sample Dialogue Tree: Talking to Tibbit**

### Ask about ancient tech

**TIBBIT**: It's elegant, impossible, and smug. Naturally I adore it.

### Ask if it's dangerous

**TIBBIT**: Everything interesting is dangerous. Chairs, for example, have ended many ambitions.

# 11. Strong One-Sentence Pitch
