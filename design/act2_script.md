# The Gilded Wake — Act 2: The Road Beneath the World

Act 2 widens the world beyond Blackwake and shifts the story from curiosity to pursuit. Act 1 was a brass key turning in a lock. Act 2 is the whole door groaning open.

## Act 2 Goals

By the end of Act 2, the player should:
* Leave the harbor region
* Follow the Wake Road inland
* Activate the first ancient relay towers
* Learn more about Rowan's past
* See Rook become an active threat instead of a stylish inconvenience
* Discover that the lost civilization did not simply hide from the world, but split over how to survive it

## OPENING CUTSCENE

Night under the cliffs. Rowan and Tibbit descend the rear lighthouse stairs onto a narrow ledge path above black water. Behind them, lantern light blooms as Rook's men force entry.

Far out at sea, an answering light glimmers through the fog. Then vanishes.

**TIBBIT**: Well. Either destiny just waved at us, or the ocean has developed sarcasm.
**ROWAN**: I'm beginning to suspect that runs in my family.

Rowan checks the Wake Road Map Plate. Three tower marks glow. The first pulses inland.

**ROWAN**: That one.
**TIBBIT**: Excellent. I was hoping fate would provide directions, because I packed enthusiasm and almost nothing useful.

## SCENE 1: UNDERCLIFF SMUGGLER PATH

### Location Description
A narrow trail cut into the cliffside below Blackwake, used by smugglers, romantics, and people avoiding customs. Rope bridges sway over surf. Rusty pulley lifts cling to stone. Old crates wedged into alcoves as if the cliff itself tried shopkeeping.

### Interactables
* Cliff lift platform, Signal lantern, Contraband crate
* Tide-slick rope bridge, Smuggler graffiti, Old speaking tube

### Examine Text
**Smuggler Graffiti**: "'NO KING BUT PROFIT.' Charming. History always begins with someone believing theft is philosophy."
**Speaking Tube**: "A pipe for shouting at distances. Blackwake's second favorite civic institution."
**Cliff Lift**: "Held together by rope, optimism, and previous owners' lies."

### PUZZLE 1: Cross the Tide Bridge

Bridge is broken halfway across. Lift counterweight is missing.

**Solution**:
1. Open contraband crate → find dense ceramic bottles
2. Load bottles into lift basket
3. Use Tibbit's coil line and hook chain to redirect lift weight to tension the bridge
4. Lock the line using lantern shutter bracket

**Wrong answers**:
* Walk across broken bridge: "I support boldness in theory. In practice, I prefer not to become a cautionary splash."
* Ask Tibbit to jump: "I'm built for invention, not ballistic decision-making."

**Payoff**: As Rowan crosses, ancient markings visible on inner cliff face — same sequence from lighthouse.
**ROWAN**: Someone built this route over something older.
**TIBBIT**: Blackwake in one sentence.

## SCENE 2: THE BRACKMARSH

### Location Description
Vast wetland of reeds, black pools, low fog, half-drowned stone causeways. Steam contraptions rust beside ruins far older than the coast. A small chapel on stilts leans over the marsh, its bell tied down to keep wind from making theology too dramatic.

### NPC: Sister Caligo
**CALIGO**: Travelers usually come here to disappear. You look like you're trying to arrive.
**ROWAN**: I'm still deciding which would be more restful.

Options:
1. "Do you know about old towers in the marsh?" → "Plenty. The marsh swallows fools, the tower confuses survivors, and both have terrible hospitality."
2. "What are those standing mirrors?" → "Not mirrors. Markers. They reflect what the fog wants you to see, which is usually nothing useful."
3. "Has anyone else come through?" → "Men in polished boots. Too clean for common sense. So yes, trouble is ahead of you."

### Examine Text
**Standing Mirrors**: "Black glass panels sunk in the mud. They look less like they were placed here and more like the earth failed to digest them."
**Bell Rope**: "A rope that says, in no uncertain terms, 'Do not make the marsh louder.'"
**Reed Skiff**: "Small, damp, and deeply committed to not being a full boat."

### PUZZLE 2: Navigate the Mirror Fog

Marsh paths obscured by unnatural fog from half-dormant relay system. Black markers can redirect fog when aligned.

**Solution**:
1. Borrow/distract Caligo to get **chapel hand mirror**
2. Ring bell with soft pull (not full force)
3. Watch which standing marker vibrates first
4. Use rusted bar to rotate each marker toward the next
5. Hold hand mirror to catch light through fog, revealing safe path

**Wrong**: Ring bell too hard → marsh erupts with birds, mud vent explodes.
**CALIGO**: Congratulations. You have summoned every creature except wisdom.

**Payoff**: Fog parts to reveal black stone tower rising from marsh like a spearhead.

## SCENE 3: THE FIRST RELAY TOWER

### Location Description
Slender ancient structure in shallow water, etched with luminous grooves. Smooth dark surfaces with few brass repairs from later scavengers. Base has circular platform with three sockets and central interface.

### Examine Text
**Relay Pedestal**: "Elegant, impossible, and one missing piece away from being rude."
**Tone Forks**: "So the ancients built towers that run on harmony. Naturally. Why use a lever when you can require emotional alignment from architecture."

### PUZZLE 3: Restore the Harmonic Circuit

Tower needs three conductor rods. Two improvised, one hidden in chapel.

**Solution**:
1. Get **brass curtain rod** from Caligo (exchange: repair her roof latch)
2. Combine reeds and wire → temporary conductor
3. Use **automaton finger** from inventory as precision conductor
4. Insert all three into sockets
5. Use **Relay Key** to begin calibration
6. Match tone sequence by selecting correct embedded forks

**Wrong sequence**: Tower emits disapproving buzz. "Good. It has opinions."
**Another wrong**: Marsh herons imitate the tone. "We have either solved the puzzle or started a very niche choir."

**Payoff**: Tower activates. Beam shoots skyward, bends horizontally through fog toward interior.

### MEMORY VISION 3
A lesson hall on the island. Young Rowan and children before a map of glowing points.
**INSTRUCTOR**: The road remains hidden until the relays agree. Harmony is not decoration. It is permission.
**YOUNG ROWAN**: And if one tower refuses?
**INSTRUCTOR**: Then the road closes. And the world remains outside.

**ROWAN**: They built the route to choose who could enter.
**TIBBIT**: Yes. Which is either security or arrogance, depending on whether it's pointed at you.

Player receives: **Transit Sigil Fragment** + **Ancient Tone Cylinder**

## SCENE 4: THE SUNKEN WAYSTATION

### Location Description
Partially submerged relay station beneath the marsh causeway. Half ancient transit stop, half drowned waiting room. Benches of impossible material. Flickering map arch. Vacuum-sealed storage lockers.

### Examine Text
**Transit Map Arch**: "The map is trying very hard to exist."
**Ticket Slot**: "A civilization advanced enough to hide an island and still cursed with access control."

### PUZZLE 4: Make the Waystation Talk

**Solution**:
1. Pump out lower chamber water
2. Dry map contacts
3. Insert **Tone Cylinder**
4. Use **Sigil Fragment** in ticket slot
5. Reconnect broken signal wire using Tibbit's coil clip

**Wrong item in ticket slot**: "I've just attempted to board an ancient transit system with a commemorative spoon. I want credit for initiative."

**Payoff**: Map shows relay active, routes to Ironwind Air Dock and Fogwound Ruins, final offshore marker. Recorded voice: "Wake sequence incomplete. Aerial transfer required. Authorized bloodline recognized. Warning: civic division remains unresolved."

Message strip ejects: "SECOND ACCESS RESTRICTED BY ORDER OF THE WESTERN COUNCIL."

**ROWAN**: There were factions.
**TIBBIT**: Naturally. No civilization ever gets shiny enough to outgrow politics.

## SCENE 5: THE IRONWIND AIR DOCK

### Location Description
Cliffside dock of derelict airships, mooring spines, wind-torn signal flags. Old world and older one scrape against each other like gears with different teeth.

### NPC: Bram Kett
Lounges under the hull of **The Patient Gull**, a patched, pear-shaped airship.

**BRAM**: No.
**ROWAN**: We haven't asked anything yet.
**BRAM**: Experience.

Options:
1. "We need a pilot." → "Then I recommend religion."
2. "We can pay." → "With what." / "Limited coin and unreasonable urgency." / "So, not the good kind of job."
3. "What if it's historic?" → "History crashes just as hard."

### Examine Text
**The Patient Gull**: "An airship with the expression of a dog that has eaten a chair."
**Mooring Winch**: "Industrial confidence in spool form."
**Fuel Gauge**: "The needle appears to be pointing at 'ambition.'"

### PUZZLE 5: Convince Bram to Fly

**Solution**:
1. Show Bram the active route map
2. Repair Patient Gull's stabilizer linkage using mooring wire + valve pin + Tibbit's "Portable Coil of Selective Certainty" clamp
3. Choose correct dialogue: "Rook will get there first if you stay here brooding."

**BRAM**: That oily peacock? Fine. I'll fly. But only because I hate being preempted by money.

### AERIAL TRANSIT CUTSCENE
Patient Gull lurches skyward. Marsh glimmers below. Relay beams converge on ruins.

Rook's airship appears, sleek and black-hulled.
**ROWAN**: He followed us.
**BRAM**: Of course he did. Villains never navigate. They pursue.

## SCENE 6: FOGWOUND RUINS OUTER COURT

### Location Description
Broken court of white stone swallowed by roots and moss. Broken archways ring central plaza. Rook's brass salvage scaffolds show his people have already begun prying into the site.

**BRAM**: I'll keep the ship warm.
**ROWAN**: That sounds optimistic.
**BRAM**: No. It's a figure of speech. The ship leaks.

### Examine Text
**Rook Camp Note**: "'Subject site exhibits sealed transit logic. Continue extraction.' There's something uniquely dreadful about greed with penmanship."

### PUZZLE 6: Bypass the Excavation Gate

Rook's men blocked main entrance. Rowan must use hidden shadow mechanism.

**Solution**:
1. Read camp note (Rook couldn't solve "light-lock sequence")
2. Position lantern on fallen statue plinth
3. Add foil reflector behind it
4. Arrange rods/frame to cast missing symbol shadow onto three rotating panels
5. Hidden passage opens in side wall

**Wrong shadow**: Creates duck silhouette.
**TIBBIT**: That seems less like a secret of the ancients and more like a personal embarrassment.

---

## SCENE 7: THE RUINS TRANSIT VAULT

### Location Description
Pristine chamber beneath the broken court. Walls glow faintly. Huge circular door ringed with dormant transit sigils. Suspended mechanism like a compass made by mathematicians who distrusted straight lines.

### Examine Text
**Split Civic Crest**: "Two halves of the same emblem, broken clean down the center. Well. That feels subtle."
**Archive Node**: "A machine specifically designed to preserve history. Which means it probably also edits it."

### PUZZLE 7: Reconcile the Split Crest

The biggest puzzle of Act 2. Transit system won't proceed because "civic division unresolved."

**Solution**:
1. Insert **Map Plate** into transit compass
2. Place **Sigil Fragment** into missing slot of split crest
3. Use **Council strip** in archive node to retrieve opposing faction seal
4. Replay **Tone Cylinder** to hear two overlapping melodies
5. Rotate suspended ring assembly until both tone patterns harmonize
6. Insert **Relay Key** to confirm

**Wrong** (favor one half): "Authorization incomplete. Civic assent imbalanced." / "Even their doors held grudges."

## ACT 2 CLIMAX: ARCHIVE REVELATION

Projection fills chamber: two island council representatives arguing.

**WESTERN COUNCILOR**: If we open the road again, the mainland will bring the old hunger with it.
**EASTERN COUNCILOR**: If we close it forever, we become a mausoleum with gardens.

Rowan's father steps forward: "Then let the road remain sleeping, but not dead. Leave a path for return."

Final line from Rowan's mother: "Send the child away. If the councils fracture, the key must survive outside the seal."

**TIBBIT**: You weren't lost.
**ROWAN**: No.
**TIBBIT**: You were hidden.
**ROWAN**: Yes.

Player receives: **Second Relay Core** + **Aerial Transit Prism** + **White Civic Signet Half**

## ROOK CONFRONTATION

Applause from chamber entrance. Rook stands there with two agents.

**ROOK**: Magnificent. I do so appreciate arriving after the difficult part.
**ROOK**: Hand over the key and I may permit you to remain a footnote in your own ancestry.
**ROWAN**: Tempting. But I've grown attached to being inconvenient.

### PUZZLE 8: Escape the Vault

Rapid multi-step escape using vault systems.

**Solution**:
1. Insert **Aerial Transit Prism** into light channel
2. Rotate compass to refract beams wildly
3. Use lantern to overload one light sensor
4. Tibbit pulls lever for false route activation
5. Transit shutter drops between Rowan and Rook
6. Escape through maintenance conduit

**Wrong** (throw teacup at Rook): "I briefly considered elegance as a weapon. I regret it already."

**ROOK**: Miss Vale!
**ROWAN**: Commodore!
**ROOK**: This is temporary.
**ROWAN**: That's true of all disappointing men.

## ACT 2 ENDING

Ruins ridge at dawn. Rowan, Tibbit, Bram regroup at Patient Gull.

Map Plate now shows: First relay active, second route unlocked, Fogwound vault active, final offshore marker stronger but incomplete.

Marrow Quill already waiting on the ridge.
**ROWAN**: Do you ever arrive normally?
**MARROW**: Only when disappointing.

**MARROW**: The island split more deeply than I feared. One council sealed the road. Another left the means to restore it.
**ROWAN**: My parents were part of that second group.
**MARROW**: Yes.
**ROWAN**: Then the island is not merely hidden. It's divided.
**MARROW**: Worse. It may have mistaken stillness for peace.

**BRAM**: Where's the next relay?
**MARROW** (pointing inland): Under the mountain. The old undersea transit chamber.
**TIBBIT**: Marvelous. From marshes and villains to caves and probable collapse.

**ROWAN**: One relay left.
**MARROW**: One relay. One final route. Then the sea will answer.

Far offshore, through fog, a faint line of white architecture is almost visible. Almost.

**END OF ACT 2**
