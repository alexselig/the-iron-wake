# The Gilded Wake — Act 1: The Thing the Tide Brought In

## Act 1 Goals

By the end of Act 1, the player should:
* Meet the core cast
* Discover Rowan's strange connection to ancient technology
* Solve a chain of escalating harbor and market puzzles
* Gain access to the old lighthouse
* Experience the first major memory fragment
* Learn that the washed-up relic is part of something much larger

## OPENING CINEMATIC

Black screen. Wind. Waves smashing timber piles. Then a metallic chime from somewhere beneath the surf.

Title card: **THE GILDED WAKE**

Camera glides over storm-battered Blackwake. Brass pipes breathe steam. Nets snap in the wind. Along the beach, townsfolk gather around something enormous embedded in the sand — smooth, black, seamless, and ancient. Its surface glows with faint geometric lines.

**DOCKMASTER PINDLE**
Stand back! No one is to touch the object until it has been documented, classified, categorized, and denied!

**FISHMONGER**
It landed in front of my stall. That makes it mine.

**PRIESTESS**
It rose from the sea like judgment.

**TIBBIT WRENCH**
It rose from the sea like engineering.

**PINDLE**
Same paperwork either way.

The crowd parts as **ROWAN VALE** approaches from the harbor lane.

**ROWAN**
Why is the entire town staring at a rock?

**TIBBIT**
Because if it were an ordinary rock, Blackwake would have priced it already.

The relic hums. A line of white-blue light sweeps across its surface and stops on Rowan. A circular recess opens, matching the medallion Rowan wears.

**PRIESTESS**
It knows them.

**ROWAN**
I deeply dislike that sentence.

**PINDLE**
By Harbor Ordinance Forty-Three, this is now a personal irregularity. Congratulations.

Fade to gameplay.

## SCENE 1: THE SHORE RELIC

### Location Description
A storm-lashed strip of pebbled sand. Kelp tangles around driftwood. Gulls patrol overhead with criminal intent. The relic sits half-buried in the shore, radiating impossible calm.

### Interactables
* Ancient Relic
* Rowan's Medallion
* Cracked Spyglass in a salvage crate
* Dockmaster's inspection stamp
* Steam crane valve on the seawall
* Warning placard: "NO UNAUTHORIZED RESONANCE EVENTS"
* Wet sand in relic recess
* Crowd of onlookers

### Primary Objective
Activate the relic.

### Examine Text

**Ancient Relic**: "Smooth, seamless, and smug. I've met wealthy people less polished than this."
**Recess in Relic**: "Something fits here. Something circular, old, and alarmingly familiar."
**Rowan's Medallion**: "I've had this since childhood. No idea where it came from. Which, in hindsight, feels less charming than it used to."
**Cracked Spyglass**: "A sailor's spyglass. Lens cracked, brass cheap, still more trustworthy than most officials."
**Inspection Stamp**: "A block of wood on a handle. The chosen scepter of tiny authority."
**Steam Valve**: "One of Blackwake's many wheels for deciding whether pipes are happy."
**Warning Placard**: "'NO UNAUTHORIZED RESONANCE EVENTS.' That's not reassuring. That's advertising."

### Dialogue: Tibbit at Shore

**TIBBIT**: You see the symmetry? The precision? The utter disdain for rivets? This thing's older than anything in town and in better condition than the mayor.

Options:
1. "What do you think it is?" → "A machine. Or a shrine. Or a machine people worshipped because they lacked the self-respect to read instructions."
2. "You seem excited." → "I live in a town where innovation means putting an extra handle on a crate. Yes, I'm excited."
3. "Should I put the medallion in?" → "Absolutely." / "You said that too quickly." / "That's how certainty sounds."

### Dialogue: Pindle at Shore

**PINDLE**: I remind everyone present that accidental awakening of maritime antiquities is subject to a filing fee.

Options:
1. "Is that a real law?" → "If I wrote it down in triplicate, yes."
2. "Can I borrow your stamp?" → "Certainly not. That stamp is for trained personnel." / "You stamp boxes." / "And yet, not everyone is qualified."
3. "What happens if this thing opens?" → "Then it becomes a customs matter. Which is worse."

### PUZZLE 1: Activate the Relic

**Logic**: The medallion fits the recess, but it's packed with wet sand and fine salt crust. The relic shocks when touched directly.

**Solution**:
1. Take the **Cracked Spyglass**
2. Use it on morning sunlight to dry the sand in the recess
3. Distract Pindle (provoke Fishmonger argument) and obtain the **Inspection Stamp**
4. Use stamp handle edge to scrape the dried sand out
5. Insert the **Medallion**
6. Pull **Steam Valve** to produce resonance tone that fully wakes the relic

**Wrong Answers**:
* Use Hand on Recess: "Ow. I'd like it noted the machine struck first."
* Use Medallion on Sandy Recess: "I'm not grinding mystery heirlooms into beach paste."
* Pull Steam Valve Too Early: Crane horn blares. Gulls erupt. "Excellent. We have successfully alarmed the seagulls."

**Getting the Stamp**: Fishmonger argues with Pindle after Rowan provokes him. During argument, stamp is unattended.
* Try to take stamp before distraction: "Not while he's looking. I'm brave, not administrative."

### Relic Activation Sequence

Relic unfolds. White lines spiral. Hovering geometric projection appears: a map fragment, crescent shoreline, symbol like a sun behind an island.

**MEMORY VISION**: White stone steps. Children running. A woman fastens the medallion on young Rowan. "When the sea remembers, you must return."

Post-vision:
**TIBBIT**: You vanished for a second.
**ROWAN**: I was standing right here.
**TIBBIT**: Physically, yes. Spiritually, your expression went somewhere expensive.

Player receives: **Engraved Brass Strip**
**ROWAN**: Good. The sea has started giving me homework.

## SCENE 2: THE CUSTOMS SHACK

### Objective
Access the Salvage Warehouse to match the engraved strip to records.

### Location Description
A tiny shack puffing steam. Inside: shelves of forms, stacks of ledgers, three teacups, one dead fern, and a desk that has achieved authority through sheer stubbornness.

### Interactables
* Permit ledger, Ink pad, Wall notices, "Official Seal" press
* Queue rope, Moisture declaration forms, Cabinet labeled "Irregularities"

### Examine Text
**Permit Ledger**: "Every page is a different variation on 'absolutely not.'"
**Wall Notices**: "'Report all sentient driftwood.' That cannot possibly have happened often enough to justify a sign."
**Official Seal Press**: "A stamp large enough to leave emotional bruising."

### Dialogue with Pindle
**PINDLE**: Warehouse access is restricted to certified inspectors, licensed salvors, and visiting relatives of municipal esteem.

Options:
1. "How does one become a certified inspector?" → "Years of training. Or a form."
2. "May I have a form?" → "No."
3. "Why not?" → "Because I know what you'd do with it." / "Fill it out?" / "Improperly."

### PUZZLE 2: Forge a Permit

**Goal**: Create a fake "Emergency Resonant Moisture Assessment" authorization.

**Needed**: Blank form + Ink + Correct jargon + Seal impression + Supporting evidence

**Clue Sources**:
* Wall notices: "Emergency Inspection", "Salt Contamination", "Resonant Cargo", "Pending Spoilage"
* Tibbit: "Try 'volatile salvage condition.' Officials love words that sound expensive and damp."

**Solution**:
1. Swipe blank **Moisture Declaration Form** when Pindle turns to reach a ledger
2. Combine form + ink + copied phrases + **Fish paper wrapper** (proof of "pending spoilage")
3. Press softened wax into **Official Seal** to create imprint
4. Present to Pindle with confidence

**Permit Presentation**:
**PINDLE**: Hm. "Emergency Resonant Moisture Assessment." "Pending spoilage." "Immediate supervised entry." This is highly irregular.
**ROWAN**: That sounds promising.
**PINDLE**: It is. No one forges this many subclauses unless they mean business.
He opens the warehouse.
**PINDLE**: You have ten minutes and one infraction.
**ROWAN**: Only one?
**PINDLE**: I'm rationing.

## SCENE 3: SALVAGE WAREHOUSE

### Location Description
Long brick warehouse. Inside: crates of storm salvage, confiscated contraptions, broken automata, relic fragments nobody understands.

### Key Interactables
* Shelves of tagged relic pieces
* Broken automaton hand
* Crate marked "Lighthouse Transfer"
* Symbol archive board
* Suspiciously polished black shard
* Chain hoist

### Examine Text
**Black Shard**: "Same material as the beach relic. Which is unfortunate, because I was hoping this morning had been unique."
**Automaton Hand**: "A metal hand. Helpful if I need a handshake from someone extremely committed to being dead."
**Lighthouse Transfer Crate**: "'HUSHLIGHT LENS HOUSING - FRAGILE.' That feels relevant in the way danger often does."
**Symbol Archive Board**: "At last. Organized theft."

### Warehouse Discovery
The engraved strip has three symbols. Archive board identifies one as **Hushlight Lighthouse**, another as **Tide Transit**, third is missing. Strip slides into something like a projector or lens mount.

**TIBBIT**: This isn't a map, exactly. More like a machine telling another machine where to point.
**ROWAN**: To the lighthouse.

### FIRST APPEARANCE: SILAS ROOK

Doors swing open. Enter Commodore Rook — navy coat, brass detailing, every button polished.

**ROOK**: I leave town for three days and the tide develops taste.

He notices Rowan holding the brass strip.

**ROOK**: That fragment belongs to my company's salvage claim.
**ROWAN**: Funny. It flew out of a machine that seemed to disagree.
**ROOK**: Machines do not own themselves.
**ROWAN**: That depends how bitey they are.

**ROOK**: Be careful with old things, Miss Vale. The past tends to cut the hand that rummages through it.
**TIBBIT**: And the present?
**ROOK**: The present sends invoices.

He leaves.
**TIBBIT**: Well. I hate him efficiently.

## SCENE 4: THE BRASS BAZAAR

### Objective
Get the missing focusing lens component for the lighthouse.

### Location Description
Cluttered maze of canopies, rope-strung lamps, dangling gears, polished junk. A mechanical parrot shouts gossip at random.

### Examine Text
**Mechanical Parrot**: "ROOK BUYS QUIETLY. ROOK PAYS POORLY." / "Finally, a bird with editorial standards."
**Pile of Copper Masks**: "Theatrical, unsettling, and somehow already judging me."
**Box of "Authentic Ancient Springs"**: "These were made last week and insultingly so."

### Dialogue with Mirelle Soot
**MIRELLE**: Welcome to Soot & Sundries. Buy quickly, doubt later.

Options:
1. "I need a lighthouse lens." → "Of course you do. Everybody does eventually. Mostly after falling."
2. "How much?" → "For you? Not money. Something rarer." / "Reasonable behavior?" / "No, I'm stocked on that."
3. "What do you want?" → "A favor in disguise. Impress me."

### PUZZLE 3: The Bazaar Bluff

Mirelle has the **Focusing Disc** but refuses coin. She wants something "prestigious enough to be pointless."

**Collectibles**: Broken Guild Badge, Fancy Teacup, gossip from parrot, noble jargon from eavesdropping.

**Without preparation**: "That badge is upside down and from a stove manufacturer."

**Correct approach**: Learn phrase "Third Conservatory Circle, provisional survey class." Use fancy teacup as status prop.

**ROWAN**: Third Conservatory Circle. Provisional survey class. We're reclaiming the Hushlight apparatus for preservation review.
**MIRELLE**: And the cup?
**ROWAN**: Status hydration.

Mirelle laughs. Player gets **Focusing Disc**.

**MIRELLE**: One warning, darling. People have started asking after those old lighthouse parts. Wealthy people. Calm people. The worst kind.

## SCENE 5: TIBBIT'S WORKSHOP CART

### Location Description
A mobile workshop: half tool chest, half explosion apology. Springs hang from hooks. One burner hisses under a pot labeled "DO NOT TASTE AGAIN."

### PUZZLE 4: Assemble the Memory Lens

**Needed**: Focusing Disc + Clock spring + Conductive salt paste (harbor brine + soot ash)

**Wrong combination** (whistle + lens):
**ROWAN**: No. Memory does not need an entrance theme.
**TIBBIT**: Counterpoint: everything improves with one.

**On completion**: Tibbit fastens last piece. Lens emits soft note.
**ROWAN**: Why did it whistle.
**TIBBIT**: Atmosphere.

Player gets: **Restored Memory Lens**

## SCENE 6: HARBOR CLIFFS PATH

Transition scene. Wind-battered path above the sea. Ancient markings on boundary stones.

**Boundary Stone**: "Same symbol family. Which means either I'm on the right path or the entire coastline has started following me."

At the top: Hushlight Lighthouse, long shuttered. **Marrow Quill** waits by the door.

**MARROW**: You came quickly.
**ROWAN**: I dislike being expected by strangers.
**MARROW**: Then you will dislike the rest of this story.

## SCENE 7: HUSHLIGHT LIGHTHOUSE EXTERIOR

### Dialogue with Marrow Quill

Options:
1. "Who are you?" → "Someone who stayed too near the shore for too long."
2. "Do you know this symbol?" → "I know what it guarded. I know what it forgot. I know what it has begun to remember."
3. "Are you going to explain anything plainly?" → "Plain explanations are for plain times." / "That's infuriating." / "Yes."

### PUZZLE 5: Open the Lighthouse Door

**Solution**:
1. Apply **salt paste** to conductive grooves
2. Insert **engraved strip**
3. Rotate exterior beacon crank to provide current

**MARROW**: It still knows the route.
**ROWAN**: Route to what.
**MARROW**: To what was hidden.

## SCENE 8: HUSHLIGHT LIGHTHOUSE MAIN CHAMBER

### Location Description
Circular chamber with broken mirrors, brass rails, tide charts, towering lens assembly. Dust in slanting light. Abandoned by people, not by purpose.

### Interactables
* Main lens pedestal, Chart table, Wall mural
* Rotating beacon controls, Hidden lower compartment
* Ancient mural ring, Window shutters

### Examine Text
**Wall Mural**: "That island again. Either fate is calling or architecture had a mascot."
**Chart Table**: "These aren't sea lanes. They're instructions."
**Beacon Controls**: "A lighthouse mechanism for people who thought 'simple' was a moral failing."

### PUZZLE 6: Align the Lens

**Solution**:
1. Open shutters for sunset light
2. Install **Restored Memory Lens** into central assembly
3. Rotate beacon mirrors to align with mural symbols
4. Use **brass strip** as selector key

**Wrong alignments**:
* Fish projection: "We have discovered... a herring." / "Do not sound pleased."
* Distorted Rowan face: "Wonderful. A haunted portrait by someone who dislikes me."

## ACT 1 CLIMAX: SECOND MEMORY VISION

Correct alignment → chamber darkens → hidden route pattern revealed beneath mural paint.

**MARROW**: The Wake Road.

Rowan touches the route. FLASH.

**Vision**: Warm sunlight. White terraces. Silver-leaf trees. Young Rowan in a courtyard.
**MAN'S VOICE**: Listen carefully. If they close the gates, you follow the road of lights. You do not wait for us.
**WOMAN'S VOICE**: The sea will hide you. The sea will bring you back.
Alarm bells. Metal doors sealing. A glowing island beyond fog. Darkness.

**Back in lighthouse**:
**ROWAN**: I remember them. I was there. That island is real.
**MARROW**: Some truths must be arrived at, not handed over.
**ROWAN**: I may throw you into the sea on principle.
**MARROW**: Get in line.

Player receives: **Relay Key** + **Wake Road Map Plate**

---

## ACT 1 ENDING

Pounding at the lighthouse door.

**ROOK**: Miss Vale! I do dislike closed doors. It suggests someone is learning without permission.
**TIBBIT**: That's him. I know villain footsteps.

Marrow points to a service stair at the rear.

**MARROW**: Go inland. Find the towers. Wake the road before Rook does.
**ROOK**: Open this tower, or I shall buy it and open it with receipts!

**ROWAN**: All right. We find the towers. We find the road. We find the island.
**TIBBIT**: And possibly lunch?
**ROWAN**: If fate permits.
**TIBBIT**: It had better. I am not solving destiny on an empty stomach.

They flee down the rear stairs. The lighthouse beam sputters once, sends a white line across the sea. Far beyond the fog, a matching light answers.

**END OF ACT 1**
